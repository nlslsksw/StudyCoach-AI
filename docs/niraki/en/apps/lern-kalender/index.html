Lern Kalender: Timetable

Timetable, homework, study time and grades in one place – for students to stay on track, for parents to stay in the loop.

Download on theApp Store

Lern Kalender brings timetable, homework, study time and grades together. Students stay on track with the study timer, streak and grade goals; parents can, if they want, see how their child is studying – without having to ask.

What Lern Kalender does

  • Study timer: with a Live Activity on the lock screen and background sound.
  • Study streak: a streak with streak ice – motivation without pressure.
  • Grades with a goal: set a target average and the app works out which grade you need next.
  • Calendar: exams, study days and school holidays for all German states.
  • Statistics & widget: week, month, year, a school-year recap and a home screen widget.

Free, with a one-time Pro purchase

The app is free for students. With Pro you unlock, once, the WebUntis connection (timetable, homework and substitutions automatically) and the parent view (dashboard, exam countdown, push to the parent's phone, study goals, motivational messages). No subscription, no ads.

Frequently asked questions

What data is stored?
Data stays on the device and in your iCloud. What parents see is transmitted encrypted – the key is the connection code and never leaves your devices. There is no account and no tracking.

Do I need WebUntis?
No. The WebUntis connection is optional; the app works without it. Lern Kalender is an independent product and is not affiliated with Untis GmbH. WebUntis is a trademark of Untis GmbH.

How do I connect a parent and a child device?
On the child's device go to Settings → Family and create a connection code, then enter it on the parent's device.

I bought the app earlier – what now?
You keep everything. "Restore Purchases" on the purchase page confirms it.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad, iOS 26.2 or later, in German and English.
