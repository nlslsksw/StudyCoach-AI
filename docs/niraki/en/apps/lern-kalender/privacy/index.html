2. Overview

The app processes only the data its features need. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

3. Data on your device

The following data is stored locally on your iPhone/iPad (UserDefaults, iCloud Key-Value Store, local file system):

  • School calendar entries (study days, exams, reminders)
  • Subjects and school years
  • Study time and statistics
  • Grades
  • Timetable, homework and attachments
  • App settings

Photos or files you attach to homework stay on your device only.

4. iCloud sync

If you use the app with your iCloud account, this data is also synced between your devices through your private iCloud account. Apple acts as processor. We have no access to it.

5. Family feature (parent ↔ child)

When you connect a parent device with a child device:

  • A 10-character connection code is generated. It exists only on the two devices, not in the cloud.
  • Certain study data of the child (study time, grades, streak, exams, subjects, timetable entries) is written encrypted (AES-GCM, key derived from the connection code) to the provider's public CloudKit database so the parent device can retrieve it. Without the code it is unreadable; lookup uses only a hash of the code.
  • The only exception is the short text of an activity notification (e.g. "Maths – 45 min studied"), which Apple must be able to read to deliver the notification. It contains neither names nor grades.
  • Parents can send study goals and motivational messages to the child.

You can disconnect at any time in Settings; the family CloudKit data is deleted when you do.

6. WebUntis connection (optional)

If you connect your timetable to WebUntis, the app communicates directly with your school's WebUntis server (Untis GmbH). Your credentials are stored encrypted in your device's keychain and are not transmitted to us. Your school's and Untis GmbH's privacy terms apply. Lern Kalender is an independent product and is not affiliated with Untis GmbH.

7. One-time purchase

The WebUntis connection and the parent view are unlocked with a single purchase. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple (App Store). We receive no payment data; the app checks the purchase status locally via StoreKit. The legal basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

8. What we do not do

  • No tracking, no tracking frameworks.
  • No advertising, no sale of data to third parties, no profiling.
  • No location data.
  • No access to contacts, system calendar, reminders or photo library beyond the photo picker you trigger yourself.

9. Permissions

  • Photos – when you pick an image
  • Notifications – for reminders and parent notifications

10. Retention

Local data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. iCloud data stays in your iCloud account. Family CloudKit data is deleted when you disconnect.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77).

12. Children

The app is suitable for students of all ages. For children under 16 we recommend use with parental consent.
